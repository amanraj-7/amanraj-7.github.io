I am a PhD student at [Arizona State University](https://www.asu.edu/)'s [School of Sustainable Engineering and the Built Environment](https://ssebe.engineering.asu.edu/), working in the [CESEM Lab](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=LV3kAbYAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao) with [Dr. Dwarakanath Ravikumar](https://search.asu.edu/profile/1869256).

I hold an MS in Sustainable Systems from [RIT](https://www.rit.edu/), where I studied lithium-ion battery remanufacturing with [Dr. Callie Babbitt](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=oZyg9b4AAAAJ&view_op=list_works&sortby=pubdate); a master's in Public Policy from the [Tata Institute of Social Sciences, Mumbai](https://tiss.ac.in/), supervised by [Dr. Chandrashekhar Vishwanath Joglekar](https://tiss.ac.in/view/9/employee/chandrashekhar-joglekar/); and a BTech in Electronics and Communication Engineering from [VIT](https://vit.ac.in/).

My primary research focus is to develop data-driven methods, combining hyperspectral imaging, machine learning, and life cycle assessment, that treat information as a resource to operationalize and improve the environmental outcomes of a circular economy for photovoltaics in the United States. See my complete publications at [Google Scholar profile](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=kHQUlTkAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao).

Since August 2026, I have lectured in Earth Systems Engineering and Management (CEE/CNE 400), an undergraduate course at Arizona State University. The course examines how sustainability challenges must be addressed beyond technology, through their social and cultural dimensions. I am also a member of the student planning committee for the [International Symposium on Sustainable Systems and Technology (ISSST)](https://issst.net/), where I help shape the conference program and student engagement activities. In addition, I serve as a graduate student assistant for the [IEEE Photovoltaic Specialists Conference (PVSC)](https://ieee-pvsc.org/PVSC54/index.php).
