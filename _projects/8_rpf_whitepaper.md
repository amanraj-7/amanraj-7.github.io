#### About the project

South Asia is home to 1.46 billion people, about 21% of the world's population, yet cross-border energy trade in the region remains limited. This project developed a white paper proposing a Regional Parliamentary Forum on Energy Cooperation and Energy Trade. Its goals were to build a political economy discourse in support of cross-border energy trade, identify champions for the cause, bring stakeholder concerns into the process from the bottom up, and use parliamentary forums to address bottlenecks to regional energy cooperation.
