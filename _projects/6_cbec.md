#### About the project

Capacity Building of Electricity Consumers in Rajasthan (CBEC) aimed to increase the participation of electricity consumers in regulatory processes. The project worked to build cooperation among consumers, consumer groups, and electricity distribution companies; improve consumers' understanding of regulatory frameworks and grievance redressal; and educate consumers on energy efficiency, decentralized renewable energy, and consumer protection.

#### Activities

- Consumer Assistance Cells in Bikaner, Chittorgarh, Jodhpur, and Sawai Madhopur districts that handled complaints and ran awareness campaigns
- Training modules on the Rajasthan power sector, tariff determination, reading electricity bills, consumer rights, and regulatory engagement
- Feeder-level studies of service delivery and consumer satisfaction
- Stakeholder roundtables, coalition meetings, and webinars on electricity governance
