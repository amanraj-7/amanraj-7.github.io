#### About the project

As India's central and state governments introduce schemes to accelerate the shift to electric vehicles, this project examined what the transition means for workers. Using Jaipur, Rajasthan, as a case study, it assessed how many and what kinds of jobs may be lost as electric vehicles are integrated at the city level, identified the new jobs and skills likely to emerge, and outlined pathways toward a just transition.

#### Activities

- Literature review and research methodology for a city-level jobs assessment
- Case study of Jaipur's transport and automotive workforce
- Webinar with Friedrich-Ebert-Stiftung India, "Boosting Green Economy: Is E-mobility the Way Forward in Rajasthan?" (December 2020)
